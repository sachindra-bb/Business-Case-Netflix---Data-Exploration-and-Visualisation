# Business-Case-Netflix---Data-Exploration-and-Visualisation

**Problem Statement**
Analyze the data and generate insights that could help Netflix in deciding which type of shows/movies to produce and how they can grow the business in different countries

**Dataset Used**
https://d2beiqkhq929f0.cloudfront.net/public_assets/assets/000/000/940/original/netflix.csv

**Tools/Libraries**
pandas, matplotlib, seaborn

**Key Findings/Visualizations**
Movies dominate the platform (~69%), but TV shows are rapidly growing, enhancing user retention.
Netflix prioritizes recent releases, especially post-2000s, to meet modern content demand.
TV-MA and TV-14 are the most common ratings, targeting adult and teen viewers.
Drama, Comedy, and Documentaries are the top genres, appealing to a wide global audience.
The USA, India, and UK are leading content producers.
Increasing trend in original TV series production to improve subscription loyalty.

**Recommendations given to Netflix**
Invest in regional content, especially in India and Brazil, to boost engagement.
Expand TV show offerings in countries where serialized content is popular.
Tailor content strategy by country and preferred ratings (e.g., more PG-13 in India).
Focus on popular genres and niche segments to enhance user satisfaction.
